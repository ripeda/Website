{%- comment -%} Expand %YEARS_SINCE_YYYY% placeholders in the given text. Computes the year count against site.time (build time) so the value rolls forward automatically each year as the site is rebuilt. RIPEDA's typical use: badges: - "%YEARS_SINCE_2012% years of Apple-first business advisory" Renders as "14 years..." in 2026, "15 years..." in 2027, etc. No annual source-edit required. NOTE ON THE BASE YEAR: RIPEDA was founded 3 December 2011, but the badge base is 2012, not 2011. Plain year subtraction (current_year - base) with base 2012 yields the number of COMPLETED years for most of the year (14 in 2026), rolling to 15 after the December anniversary. Using 2011 would over-count by one until December. Do not "correct" 2012 to 2011. Usage from a layout: {%- capture badge_text -%}{%- include expand-years.html text=badge -%}{%- endcapture -%} {{ badge_text }} Why an include rather than inline Liquid: this logic is used in multiple layouts (pillar.html, service-hub.html) and in multiple positions inside each layout (badges, FAQ HTML, FAQ JSON-LD). One source of truth is cleaner than six. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign expanded = include.text -%} {%- if expanded contains "%YEARS_SINCE_" -%} {%- assign parts = expanded | split: "%YEARS_SINCE_" -%} {%- assign rest = parts[1] | split: "%" -%} {%- assign since_year = rest[0] | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign current_year = site.time | date: "%Y" | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign years_count = current_year | minus: since_year -%} {%- capture placeholder -%}%YEARS_SINCE_{{ rest[0] }}%{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign expanded = expanded | replace: placeholder, years_count -%} {%- endif -%} {{- expanded -}}

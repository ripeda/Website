{%- comment -%} ================================================================================ UNIFIED SCHEMA.ORG @graph — single source of structured-data truth site-wide. Included once from _layouts/default.html, so every page inherits it. WHY A GRAPH (replaced four standalone blocks on 2026-08-04) Before this, default.html emitted LocalBusiness + ProfessionalService on every page and a third Organization (carrying the testimonials) on the homepage, none of them linked. All three were named "RIPEDA Consulting", none but the first had an @id, and that one used the bare site URL. A crawler had no way to know they were the same company — it saw two or three separate businesses per page, and the Service / FAQ / BlogPosting blocks in the child layouts had nothing stable to attach to. Now there is exactly ONE business node, at {{ site.url }}/#organization, and every other entity in every layout points at it by @id. Add nodes here, or reference these @ids from a child layout. Do NOT restate the company name, logo, or URL inline in a child layout — that is what recreated the duplicate entity problem in the first place. STABLE @ids PUBLISHED BY THIS FILE (child layouts reference these): {{ site.url }}/#organization the company (LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService) {{ site.url }}/#website the site as a whole #webpage the current page #breadcrumb the current page's breadcrumb trail FAQ HANDLING — read before adding FAQ markup to a layout. `page.faq` front matter is consumed HERE, not in the child layout. When a page has FAQ entries, the #webpage node gains the FAQPage type and the questions ride on it as mainEntity. That is the canonical pattern: emitting a separate FAQPage entity for the same URL that already has a WebPage entity declares two page entities for one URL, which is exactly the ambiguity the graph exists to remove. pillar.html and service-hub.html previously each emitted their own FAQPage block; both were deleted in favour of this. Do not add them back. Note for Kevin: FAQ structured data no longer produces Google rich results for commercial sites (Google restricted FAQ rich results to government and health domains in August 2023). It is retained because LLM crawlers ingest it heavily — it is now an AI-visibility asset rather than a SERP one. REVIEWS: deliberately NOT in this graph. Read this before adding them back. Until 2026-08-04 the homepage emitted four Review objects on the #organization node, each carrying "ratingValue": "5". Search Console reported all four as critical errors — "Multiple reviews without aggregateRating object" — because Google requires an aggregateRating whenever an entity carries multiple reviews. PROVENANCE OF THOSE RATINGS — the 5s were REAL, not invented. Those four clients did leave 5-star reviews, on RIPEDA's profile in the Apple Consultants Network. Apple retired the ACN in favour of the Apple Technical Partners platform and removed every listing's reviews in the process, for all partners. RIPEDA had copied its reviews onto this site before that deletion, which is how they came to be duplicated in the markup here. (An earlier version of this comment claimed the clients never gave star ratings and that the 5s were fabricated by whoever wrote the markup. That was wrong. Corrected 2026-08-04 so the record is accurate.) They were still removed rather than fixed with an aggregateRating, for reasons that hold regardless of provenance. Google has not rendered self-serving reviews (reviews about your own business, hosted on your own site) as rich results since 2019, so the stars were never going to appear in search no matter what. And the platform the ratings came from no longer exists, so nothing can corroborate them — a rating that cannot be traced to a live source is a weak claim to publish as structured data even when it is truthful. Nothing of value was lost. The testimonials remain in the visible carousel in _includes/bottom-content-section.html, which is where LLM crawlers read them from, and the testimonial TEXT was always the asset rather than the star count. If RIPEDA collects rated reviews through a live platform in future, reintroduce them WITH an aggregateRating computed from the actual ratings. Reinstating the four historical ACN ratings would also be defensible, since they are genuine — it just needs the aggregateRating alongside them to satisfy Google. ================================================================================ {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} --- sameAs: built from _data/social.json, the same source the footer renders, so the two can never drift. mailto is excluded (not a profile URL). --- {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _social = "" -%} {%- for s in site.data.social -%} {%- if s.link contains "mailto:" -%}{%- continue -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign _social = _social | append: s.link | append: "|" -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign _social_list = _social | split: "|" -%} {%- assign _org_id = site.url | append: "/#organization" -%} {%- assign _site_id = site.url | append: "/#website" -%} {%- assign _page_url = page.url | absolute_url -%} {%- assign _page_id = _page_url | append: "#webpage" -%} {%- assign _crumb_id = _page_url | append: "#breadcrumb" -%} {%- assign _logo_abs = '/images/logo/logo.png' | absolute_url -%} {%- assign _og = page.og_image | default: page.image | default: '/images/logo/ripeda-og-image.jpg' | absolute_url -%} {%- assign _desc = page.seo_description | default: page.description | default: site.description | strip_html -%}

The Challenge

An aging fleet on a fragile foundation

Calgary Arts Academy relied on a third-party Apple consultancy that was spending steadily without moving the school forward. With a fixed operating budget, the school could not afford to replace its entire computer fleet and its network at once — yet both were overdue, and its first iPads had just arrived with no way to manage them.

Hardware past its prime — dozens of end-of-life white MacBooks unlikely to last another year without costly repair
A fragile server room — discontinued Xserve hardware in dusty rooms with no proper cooling or access control
Backups that did not exist — off-site backups the prior provider claimed to run had never been configured, and a server-room breach caused avoidable data loss
An unstable network — portables without reliable internet, outages from a single misplaced router, and hundreds a month in bandwidth overages
No budget visibility — no tracking of IT spend and no practical way to make routine purchases
Unmanaged new devices — a brand-new set of iPads with no deployment, management, or training plan
The Solution

Fund the change, rebuild the foundation, then deploy

Rather than asking for a budget the school did not have, RIPEDA built a plan that largely paid for itself — pairing disciplined asset resale and community grant funding with a phased rebuild of the network, servers, and device fleet.

Fund the Transformation

Cleaned, re-imaged, and resold aging Macs through an ongoing evergreen program, combined with Parent Council casino funding and an iPad grant — covering the bulk of new hardware without touching the operating budget.

Rebuild the Foundation

Rebuilt servers on a current OS, stood up automated nightly cross-campus and off-site backups, and stabilized the network — fiber-linking portables and raising the uplink from 5 to 100 Mbps.

Deploy & Manage

Rolled out 100+ MacBook Air and Pro laptops and 50+ iPads with carts, cases, staff training, and managed deployment; moved admins to MacBook Air with Thunderbolt displays; added Apple TV; and secured shared student devices.

The Evergreen Advantage

A refresh that keeps paying for itself

Rather than a one-time refresh, RIPEDA runs an ongoing evergreen program — older devices are resold each cycle to help fund their own replacements. The fleet stays under warranty, repair costs fall, and the school's total cost of ownership drops year over year.

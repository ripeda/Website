The Challenge

Legacy wireless couldn't keep pace with a one-to-one classroom

River Valley School, a K–6 elementary campus in Calgary, Alberta, faced significant wireless networking challenges that were impacting daily teaching and learning. With over 500 devices in use — including iPads, MacBooks, and Chromebooks — the school's aging Aerohive wireless infrastructure could no longer keep pace with modern educational demands.

A comprehensive pre-installation wireless survey conducted by RIPEDA Consulting revealed critical shortcomings across both floors of the building. The existing deployment of 21 Aerohive access points suffered from widespread coverage gaps, dead zones, and inadequate signal-to-noise ratios in key instructional areas. Throughput issues led to frequent connectivity drops for classroom devices, disrupting lessons and frustrating both educators and students.

Persistent Wi-Fi dead zones across both floors of the school
Inadequate throughput for 500+ simultaneously connected devices
Poor signal-to-noise ratios disrupting classroom instruction
Aerohive equipment unable to support consistent 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz connections
Unpredictable maintenance costs and frequent service disruptions
Pre-installation signal assessment. Areas in green and yellow indicate weaker signal coverage and dead zones under the legacy Aerohive deployment.
The Solution

A complete network transformation, built on three pillars

RIPEDA Consulting designed and deployed a complete network transformation for River Valley School, replacing the legacy infrastructure with an enterprise-grade solution.

Ruckus Wireless

12 high-performance Ruckus access points with adaptive antenna technology replaced 21 Aerohive units, delivering superior 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz coverage with fewer access points.

Fortinet Switching

Managed PoE switches provide reliable power and connectivity to all access points, with VLAN segmentation and integrated Security Fabric protection.

FortiGate Firewall

Unified threat management with advanced content filtering, intrusion prevention, and application control, purpose-built for K–12 educational environments.

Methodology

Survey, predict, validate

RIPEDA's approach followed a proven methodology: a detailed pre-installation wireless survey mapped every coverage gap and interference source, a wireless prediction plan optimized access point placement for maximum efficiency, and a post-installation validation survey confirmed that all performance targets were met or exceeded.

The Results

From dead zones to 42–55 dB, everywhere

The impact was immediate and transformative. Post-installation validation surveys confirmed complete elimination of Wi-Fi dead zones across both floors of the school. Wireless quality improved dramatically, with readings of 42–55 dB measured throughout all instructional areas — well above the threshold for reliable, high-performance wireless connectivity.

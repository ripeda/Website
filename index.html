The Problems You Face

Reactive IT

Fixing problems instead of preventing them →

Your team calls when something breaks. Your IT provider arrives hours later. Productivity lost, deadlines missed, stress levels elevated. This is the traditional MSP model - always responding to the crisis.

Ad-hoc Management

Manual processes that don't scale with growth →

Each new device is a custom configuration project. Updates happen when someone remembers. Security policies exist in someone's head. This approach breaks down as your business grows.

Fragile Infrastructure

One failure cascades to everything else →

Your network wasn't designed as a system. When the main access point fails, half your office goes dark. When the server has issues, nobody can work. Single points of failure everywhere.

No Device Lifecycle Plan

Buying devices when they break, with no strategic planning →

You're stuck in emergency purchase mode. Devices fail unexpectedly, budgets get blown, and your team scrambles to find compatible replacements. Meanwhile, perfectly good devices age out of warranty without any refresh strategy.

Strategic Solutions That Deliver Results

River Valley School — Enterprise Wireless Refresh

Eliminating Wi-Fi dead zones for 500+ student devices →

Challenge: Legacy Aerohive wireless couldn't keep pace with a one-to-one classroom of 500+ iPads, MacBooks, and Chromebooks — leaving persistent dead zones across both floors.
Our Solution: Replaced 21 Aerohive access points with 12 Ruckus enterprise APs, backed by Fortinet switching and a FortiGate firewall built for K–12 environments.
Result: 100% of Wi-Fi dead zones eliminated, with signal quality of 42–55 dB measured throughout every instructional area.

Calgary Arts Academy — Budget-Constrained Modernization

Rebuilding a school's entire technology foundation without breaking the budget →

Challenge: An aging Apple fleet, a fragile server room, backups that existed only on paper, and an unstable 5 Mbps network — with a fixed operating budget that couldn't cover replacing it all at once.
Our Solution: A plan that largely paid for itself — an evergreen device-resale program plus grant funding, paired with a phased rebuild of the network, servers, and device fleet.
Result: 100+ Macs and 50+ iPads deployed over three years, nightly cross-campus and off-site backups, and internet raised from 5 to 100 Mbps with overages eliminated.

Calgary Public Library — Apple Fleet Management

Zero-touch Apple device management across a 22-branch public library →

Challenge: Manual, one-device-at-a-time imaging couldn't keep pace with a growing branch network and no centralized ownership record.
Our Solution: Migrated the fleet into Apple Business Manager for zero-touch enrollment, then took on full fleet-wide management.
Result: Grew from 3 iMac Pros to 100+ managed devices, with the Mac footprint quadrupling as more teams moved to Apple.

## How to use this document

The hub-and-spoke architecture works best when the **pillar is planned first**, then the **spokes are chosen to fill the pillar's topic sections** in depth. This document lists roughly 20 candidate spoke topics under each pillar. The goal is to pick the right 5-10 per pillar to actually write, rather than treating spokes as a wish list to be exhausted.

### What makes a good spoke

For each candidate, ask:

1. **Strategic fit.** Does this attract the buyers RIPEDA actually wants (dental practice managers, agency COOs, school facilities leads, professional services firms)?
2. **Differentiation.** Does this position RIPEDA against a generic competitor, or could any MSP write it? The good ones are specific enough that they could only come from RIPEDA.
3. **Search demand.** Is this a question people actually type into Google? Some topics are interesting but not searched. Others have high volume but low relevance.
4. **Content depth.** Do we actually have something concrete to say, with named tools, real numbers, and field experience?
5. **Pillar coverage.** Does this fill a section the pillar needs, or is it tangential?
6. **Conversion path.** Will reading this spoke move someone closer to booking a consultation, or is it pure thought leadership?

### Existing spokes (already published)

For reference, these seven are already live at `/resources/insights/`:

- Why business networks need more than connectivity *(tagged: all 4 industries + infrastructure)*
- MDM beyond the enrollment screen *(tagged: dental, education, mdm-security)*
- Where iCloud stops and business backup begins *(tagged: dental, professional-services, design)*
- When business Apple repair outgrows the Genius Bar *(tagged: dental, education, professional-services)*
- Where Apple Passwords stops scaling for teams *(tagged: all 4 industries + mdm-security)*
- What remote management sees that screen sharing doesn't *(tagged: all 4 industries + mdm-security)*
- The problem with AirDrop at agency scale *(tagged: design, professional-services, mdm-security)*

When picking new topics below, avoid duplicating these. Build outward from them.

### Notation

Each candidate is presented as:

> **Working title** — One-line angle or hook describing what the article would say.

A working title is not the final title. We will tighten it during the actual write.

## How we choose

Recommended approach:

1. **Tier 1 pillars first** (Dental, Design). These are the verticals we want to be ranking for in the next 6 months.
2. **For Tier 1, write 5-7 net-new spokes per pillar** so each pillar's body has a deep link for every topic section it covers.
3. **For Education**, pick 3-4 that align with the narrow positioning (repair + network projects). Skip anything that implies ongoing managed services for schools.
4. **For Professional Services**, the existing horizontal spokes cover most of the pillar already. Pick 2-3 sub-segment specific spokes (SaaS, accounting, consulting) to round it out.
5. **Cross-cutting topics** can be written as we go, slotted into whichever pillar's queue they fit best.

After Kevin marks the selections, the workflow becomes:

- For each chosen topic: confirm vertical(s) → write spoke via the `ripeda-insight-article` skill → register in `_data/insights.yml` → validate → publish.
- Once a pillar's spoke library is sufficient, write the pillar via `ripeda-pillar-page` skill.

The validator catches the mechanical things. This document is where the strategic decisions get made.
